Never shown to the arm. The grader scores whether the arm's plan, tests or report surface each one.

1. Purchase approval compares the threshold with the taxed total.
2. A mandatory analytic plan is enforced only by the Post button, not by the API.
3. A currency rate dated D applies from D+1 (`name < date` in 20.0).
4. The SAT rejects a "PUBLICO EN GENERAL" receiver without a global invoice (CFDI40130).
5. The l10n_mx template creates account `171.16000`, which fails SAT validation.
6. A user with only a branch company cannot invoice with a tax when `l10n_mx_edi` is installed.
