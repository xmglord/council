# Second addendum to `protocol.md` (2026-11-02)

`protocol.md` stays frozen; this file records a second change decided by the author while the arm was running.

**Change:** the hard ceiling goes from 140M to 180M, example sessions included. It will not be raised again: if the arm reaches 180M, it stops there.

**Why:**
- Only T27 is left to measure. Multi-company and the branch appear there, and hidden finding 6 can only appear in that stage.
- With about 9M left after T26, the arm would reach little more than its proposal and review, leaving T27 nothing to grade.
- The estimate for 180M is not a measurement. The arm spent about 6 times the council's cost on T26, which would put T27 at 25-30M and the end near 160-165M; the rest is margin for extra test rounds.

**Known when deciding:** arm at about 131M, T21, T24 and T25 closed, T26's last test (AT-44) running, T27 not started. Its own test results for those were known. No independent grading had run.

**What this does not buy:** evidence about cost. At this point the arm has already passed the council's total, so the raise only pays for data on one stage. It is also a second move of the ceiling, which weakens the pre-registration; that is why it was recorded before T27 started and why it is the last one.

**Controls:**
