# Addendum to `protocol.md` (2026-10-31)

`protocol.md` stays frozen; this file records one change decided by the author while the arm was running.

**Change:** the hard ceiling goes from 80M to 140M, example sessions included.

**Why:**
- The 80M was twice the council's cost at T21, not at the end of its build. Counted the same way as the arm (planning, build and example sessions), the council's full run cost about 91M, about 12M of it estimated, so it would not have fit either. 140M is 1.5 times that.
- At 80M the arm would stop before T26 and T27. Hidden findings 4, 5 and 6, plus roles, stamping and multi-company, can only appear there, so those metrics would be unmeasured.

**Known when deciding:** arm at 68.92M, T21 and T24 closed, T25 in its second test round. Its own test results for those were known. No independent grading had run.

**Controls:**
- The results report both points: where the arm stood at 80M and where it ended.
- The grader receives this addendum.
- Other rules are unchanged, including the continuation rule and the stop at the ceiling.
